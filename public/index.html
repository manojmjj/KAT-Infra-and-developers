Driving Heavy Infrastructure, Mining Fleet & Land Development from Gummidipoondi

KAT Infra And Developers was established in 2017 as a sole proprietorship in Gummidipoondi, Tamil Nadu, under the direct leadership of Mr. T. Vinayaga Perumal. Serving South India's industrial belt along the NH-16 corridor, the enterprise unites heavy machinery deployment with turnkey civil infrastructure and approved land development.

KAT Infra supplies high-tonnage earthmoving vehicles to iron ore mines, develops gated plotted community layouts, executes turnkey civil engineering contracts, and prepares industrial project sites with precision laser-guided earthworks.

Frequently Answered Inquiries

Key facts and direct answers on our heavy mining fleet, approved plotted layouts, and civil execution.

What core services does KAT Infra & Developers deliver?
KAT Infra & Developers operates across three dedicated divisions: (1) Heavy Mining Fleet Supply providing Komatsu and CAT hydraulic excavators and BharatBenz 2828 mining tippers to iron ore mines, (2) Approved Gated Plotted Developments and contiguous land aggregation in Gummidipoondi, Tamil Nadu, and (3) Turnkey Civil Infrastructure & Laser-Guided Site Grading for industrial facilities.
Who is the proprietor of KAT Infra and where is the headquarters?
KAT Infra & Developers is a registered Sole Proprietorship established in 2017 founded and led by Mr. T. Vinayaga Perumal. The enterprise is headquartered at KAT Towers, NH-16 Corridor, Gummidipoondi, Tiruvallur District, Tamil Nadu 601201 (Active GSTIN: 33ADAPV2790H1ZG).
What heavy vehicles are available for iron ore mines?
Our heavy earthmoving fleet includes high-tonnage Komatsu PC300-8 (31-ton) and Caterpillar 20T–32T hydraulic excavators, alongside heavy-duty BharatBenz 2828 mining tippers (18–20 Cu.M capacity). All equipment is deployed with certified 24/7 operators for pit stripping, overburden removal, and mine haulage.
What features are included in Gummidipoondi plotted developments?
Our approved plotted layouts feature modern grand entrance arches, 30 to 40 ft wide paved blacktop avenues, concrete curbs, clear stone plot demarcation, underground utilities, and 100% verified clear-title patta, chitta, and local panchayat/DTCP compliance.
How can clients contact KAT Infra for tenders or site visits?
You can reach our corporate desk directly via telephone at +91 98405 09896, email vinayagaperumal.vp1@gmail.com, or visit our headquarters at KAT Towers, NH-16, Gummidipoondi, Tamil Nadu.
